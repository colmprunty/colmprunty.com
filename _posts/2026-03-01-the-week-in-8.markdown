Short stories always go down quick, regardless of the overall combined length, so I'm now on **Amusing Ourselves to Death** by **Neil Postman**. It's about how the move away from literacy in America has led to a reduction in not only the quality of the public discourse, but the how video-based content is shaped by the form itself and is not only (arguably) inferior to the written word, but fundamentally different. Also it was written 40 years ago. Lucky Mr. Postman did not live to see TikTok.
